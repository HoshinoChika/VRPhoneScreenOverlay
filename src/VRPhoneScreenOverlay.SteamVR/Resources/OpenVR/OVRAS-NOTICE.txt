OpenVrPlayspaceMotion.cs and the release velocity calculation in
OpenVrPlayspaceDragController.cs adapt updateHandDrag/updateGravity/clampVelocity
from src/tabcontrollers/MoveCenterTabController.cpp to C#.
Modified by VRPhoneScreen Overlay contributors, 2026-10-04.
Preserved: drag-derived velocity, fling strength, time-scaled friction,
per-axis 50 m/s terminal velocity, Euler gravity, zero-offset floor touchdown.
Differences: bounded timing, tracking/input interruption cancellation, no saved
momentum/reverse gravity, fresh-origin full reset and optional planar reset.
The application retains its existing input actions and does not import bindings.
